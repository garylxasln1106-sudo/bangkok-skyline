泰国 BD：两家酒店，一个月
English voiceover / 中英双语文稿

01 · 00:00 · TWO HOTELS. ONE MONTH. / 两家酒店，一个月
One month. Two priority hotels each. Real commercial results.
一个月，每人攻坚两家重点酒店。 拿到实实在在的合作成果。

Over the past two weeks, we visited more than forty hotels together.
过去两周， 我们一起拜访了四十多家酒店。

Thank you for your commitment. Now, let's turn that effort into stronger partnerships.
谢谢大家的投入和行动力。 接下来，把努力转化为更好的合作。


02 · 00:16 · EARN THEIR SUPPORT / 给酒店支持我们的理由
Competition is intense. Hotels are tightening B2B and B2C channel controls.
市场竞争激烈。 酒店对 B2B 和 B2C 的渠道管控越来越严。

Low-season offers often go straight to OTAs. In peak season, inventory matters just as much as price.
淡季优惠，酒店常常直接给到 OTA。 旺季，拿到库存和拿到好价格同样重要。

Beyond distribution, why should the hotel support us?
除了分销， 酒店为什么要支持我们？


03 · 00:34 · MATCH THE HOTEL / 匹配不同酒店
Match the approach to the hotel.
根据酒店情况，匹配合作方式。

Hotels with a strong business, marketing-minded leaders and a budget are candidates for paid programs and events.
经营基础好、管理层认可营销， 又有预算的酒店，可以推进付费项目和活动。

For hotels with limited budgets, exchange sales and marketing support for better rates and inventory.
对预算有限的酒店， 用销售与营销支持，换取更好的价格和库存。


04 · 00:50 · PROVE AND DIFFERENTIATE / 证明产量，建立特色
Bring booking history and partnership results. Prove that we can generate business.
带上历史订单和合作成果。 证明我们能够带来生意。

Then sharpen the hotel's positioning: location, brand, product, service, and genuine care for guests.
再帮助酒店明确定位： 地段、品牌、产品、服务，以及对客人的关怀。

Turn these strengths into useful, engaging stories across social media, the hotel website and other channels.
把这些优势变成有趣、有用的内容， 持续传播到社媒、酒店官网和其他渠道。


05 · 01:11 · FOCUS THE RESOURCES / 集中资源攻坚
Shortlist candidates from your priority list. Then choose two hotels to focus on.
先从自己的重点酒店名单里筛选， 再选定两家集中攻坚。

Marketing support will prioritize these cases.
营销资源会优先支持这些标杆酒店。

Within one month, deliver verifiable progress: better rates, confirmed inventory, or other key commercial commitments.
一个月内，拿到可验证的阶段成果： 更好的价格、确认的库存，或其他关键合作条件。


06 · 01:28 · REPORT TWO LINES / 两条线汇报进展
Every week, report two things.
每周，围绕两条线汇报。

Relationships: who are the key contacts, what changed, and what happens next?
关系推进：关键人是谁， 有什么实质进展，下一步怎么做？

Commercial terms: where do rates and inventory stand? What is blocked, and what support do you need?
条件推进：价格和库存谈到了哪一步？ 卡在哪里，需要什么支持？


07 · 01:43 · KEEP A STEADY RHYTHM / 保持工作节奏
Monday to Thursday: aim for two to three hotel sales calls a day.
周一到周四： 原则上每天保持两到三个酒店销售拜访。

Friday is mainly for office work: follow-up calls, visit notes and business data.
周五原则上以办公室工作为主： 电话跟进、整理拜访记录和业务数据。

Review the week and plan the next one.
复盘本周，安排下周重点。


08 · 01:57 · REVIEW AND FOLLOW THROUGH / 固定复盘，推动落实
Reviews are Monday, Wednesday and Friday: five p.m. Thailand time, six p.m. China time.
固定周一、周三、周五复盘： 泰国时间十七点，中国时间十八点。

Note leads, records the meetings, and prepares written progress, decisions and next actions.
由 Note 主持，会议录音后， 整理进展、结论和下一步行动。

Cover business results, showcase hotels and projects, including CGTR, Chiang Mai fund recovery and tourism board initiatives.
复盘经营数据、标杆酒店及项目， 包括 CGTR、清迈酒店款项追回和旅游局事项。


09 · 02:19 · MY SUPPORT. YOUR ACTION. / 我的支持，你的行动
I will keep joining hotel visits and trips, bringing market insights and helping advance key deals.
我会继续一起拜访、出差， 收集市场信息，协助推动重点合作。

Our specialist team and our own content work will support your selected hotels.
公司专业团队与我们共同策划的内容， 会重点支持大家选定的酒店。

We will clarify reporting, teamwork and assistant roles in writing.
我也会把汇报、内部协作和助理分工写清楚。

Choose your two hotels. Bring a clear plan. Let's deliver results together.
选定你的两家酒店。 带上明确计划，我们一起拿结果。
